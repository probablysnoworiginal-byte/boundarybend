- Player attention is a mechanic.
- Knowledge should be acquired, not merely unlocked.
- Prefer "have yet" over "cannot."
- The player should engage with the world directly when possible.
- Information gathering should be gameplay.
- Inputs express player intent; the world supplies the context-appropriate action.
- One input may produce different actions when the context is readable and every result remains a reliable means to the same intended end.
- Contextual verbs are welcome. Contextual surprises are not.

These actions need to enrich the player experience, not separate the players into those who do and those who do not. Only those who have and those who have yet.
