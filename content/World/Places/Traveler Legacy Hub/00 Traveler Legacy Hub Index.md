# Traveler Legacy Hub

> [!warning] Working name
> “Traveler Legacy Hub” is a descriptive label, not an established in-world name.

The game's hub location is the clearest surviving legacy of [[World/Cosmology/The Original Travelers|the Original Travelers]]. It is inhabited by the people culturally closest to them, yet those inheritors show little practical interest in continuing the Travelers' work of stabilizing the world.

The Travelers positioned it at what they determined to be the Greater Realm's most stable point. This makes the hub unusually resilient to the current crisis, but not permanently safe: its protections were selected for an older problem by a different people.

## Core irony

The Original Travelers crossed realities, met unfamiliar peoples, stabilized the Greater Realm, and left pieces of themselves behind. Their closest cultural inheritors now consume information about the world's problems while remaining safely insulated from them.

They may care sincerely in the moment. They read news, express grief, display solidarity, and perform the setting's equivalents of social-media mourning. Their emotion rarely becomes sustained responsibility.

This is generational drift rather than a secret curse. People do not automatically want their ancestors' work any more than a child necessarily wants their parent's profession.

## Tonal compass

> **A Fallout vault meets social-media culture.**

- The population is naturally and culturally protected.
- The outside world is visible primarily as information and content.
- Inherited security makes existential danger feel remote.
- Public feeling is common; meaningful action is exceptional.
- Ancient prestige survives more strongly than ancient understanding.

## Governance

There is little conventional government. Authority feels more like an HOA than a state: it protects norms, presentation, property, comfort, and the continuity of an already secure community.

This arrangement is possible because the population's protection is deeply established. The institution manages life inside the shelter rather than exercising sovereign power across a dangerous territory.
