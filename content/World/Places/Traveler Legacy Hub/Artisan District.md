# Artisan District

The Artisan District is a developing proposal for a self-contained production neighborhood inside the [[00 Traveler Legacy Hub Index|Traveler Legacy Hub]]. It is not a single factory. Individual specialists operate dangerous machines, chemicals, kilns, pressure systems, or other high-consequence tools to make things for themselves, their families, their community, or trade.

## The memorable absence

The district should ordinarily be loud, smoky, crowded, and visibly productive. A shutdown therefore reads immediately through absence: no workers, no machine rhythm, no smoke, and no ordinary risk-management rituals.

This lets “nothing is happening here” become a conspicuous authored situation instead of an empty level-design failure.

## Embodied access

One proposed event carries a non-humanoid [[World/Peoples/Fish Folk|Fish Folk]] artisan through the district in a portable habitat tank, then transfers her into a more secure work body or station. The accommodation is not a cure and does not make her humanoid. It lets her actual body safely direct tools in an environment built around hazards she cannot otherwise cross.

Her goal is ordinary and specific: build something useful to bring home to her nation. The event should therefore communicate competence, trade, and material participation—not turn her body into the district's mystery.

## Open design pressure

- What shut the district down?
- Which hazards are ordinary workplace facts, and which belong to the crisis?
- Is the secure work body personal equipment, shared infrastructure, or a particular artisan's invention?
- How does co-op transport preserve the artisan's agency rather than reducing her to carried cargo?
- Which affordances make the district interesting even when no crisis is active?
