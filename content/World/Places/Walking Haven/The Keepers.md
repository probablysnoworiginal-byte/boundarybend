# The Keepers

The Keepers are small, cartoonish, gnome-like workers who maintain the Walking Haven. **Gnomes** is the current working name for their people, although the exact relationship between gnomes and the Keeper occupation remains open. Their culture resembles an ordinary office workforce more than an order of mystical caretakers.

## Not short humans

Gnomes must feel fundamentally different from the Haven's three common, biologically grounded peoples. Their height should not be the first or only meaningful distinction. Appearance, movement, behavior, presence, or biology must provide many earlier signals that the observer has encountered something genuinely unfamiliar.

Outside an environment where Keepers are accepted, a chance gnome encounter should register as a **cryptid sighting**. The witness sounds unbelievable when describing it; the event cannot be dismissed as having seen an unusually short human.

The actual traits producing this effect have not yet been selected. See [[World/Peoples/Gnomes|Gnomes]].

## Maintenance instead of miracles

Outsiders see them overseeing one of the greatest wonders ever created. The Keepers see maintenance schedules.

- Where citizens see a miracle, they see a functioning system.
- Where the city remembers an anniversary, they see firmware behaving correctly.
- Where visitors feel awe, they discuss filters, hydraulic pressure, efficiency, and stability.

Their worldview has gradually aligned with the machine's. If happiness remains high and the Haven continues functioning, everything is proceeding as expected.

## Tone and temperament

The Keepers are not cold, cynical, or dismissive. They simply do not share the reverence other people feel. They are the janitors of a living miracle, and to them it is another Tuesday.

Their origin, authority, relationship with the city's personality, biology, and place among the world's peoples remain to be established.
