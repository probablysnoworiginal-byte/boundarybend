# The Talent Scouts

The Talent Scouts are a proposed faction representing a fourth relationship to the Walking Haven. They were previously called “Travelers” or “Haven Travelers,” but that name collided with [[World/Cosmology/The Original Travelers|the Original Travelers]].

> [!warning] Name distinction
> The Talent Scouts are completely unrelated to the Original Travelers who once stabilized the Greater Realm. “Travelers” was only a working name for this Haven faction and has been replaced to prevent false association.

- Most residents eventually understand the Haven as home.
- [[The Keepers]] understand it as infrastructure.
- Outsiders often understand it as a city of criminals or a wonder.
- Talent Scouts understand it as a **route**.

How organized the group is has not been decided.

## A life aboard, followed by departure

Members intentionally spend decades aboard the Haven before leaving again. Their purpose is not to preserve the city or reproduce its culture elsewhere. They carry what they have learned into places that might otherwise remain culturally isolated.

They are neither conquerors nor missionaries. Their influence travels through ordinary life rather than organized expansion:

- a construction technique;
- a recipe;
- a children's game;
- a medical practice;
- a proverb; or
- an artistic style.

## Carriers, not exporters

Everything Talent Scouts carry changes. Ideas are adapted to local needs, misunderstood, combined with existing traditions, or separated from their original purpose.

Over centuries, descendants of borrowed ideas become ordinary parts of other societies. Their origins disappear even while the practices survive.

Talent Scouts therefore do not export a stable Walking Haven culture. They participate in [[World/Foundations/Cultural Transmission|cultural evolution]], allowing distant civilizations to share subtle similarities without implying that the Haven dominates them.

## Historical role

Together with the Haven's uninterrupted existence, the Talent Scouts help make the city the world's connective tissue. The city provides a continuous meeting place; Talent Scouts and countless less intentional migrants carry changed pieces away.

Whether the Talent Scouts understand this long-term role, record it, or simply live it remains open.
