# The Walking Haven

The Walking Haven is an ancient mobile city built around one purpose: **offer refuge**.

It was not intended to become a nation, empire, or religion. It is infrastructure on a colossal scale—a machine that travels the world and opens its gates to anyone who needs somewhere to go. It asks almost nothing, does not judge the past, and does not decide who deserves another chance.

Thousands of years of unconditional welcome produced an accidental civic culture. Criminals, dissidents, heretics, failures, exiles, revolutionaries, healers, former heroes, and ordinary people with nowhere else to belong gradually became neighbors.

## The heart of the concept

- To outsiders, it is a city of criminals.
- To its citizens, it is the only place that accepted them.
- To [[The Keepers|the Keepers]], it is an infrastructure project with another filter due for replacement.
- To the city itself, it is simply home.

The Walking Haven does not believe it is saving the world. It believes it is taking care of its neighbors.
