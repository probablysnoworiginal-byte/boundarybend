# The Growth Current

The **growth current** is something stable that manifests within reality's naturally uneven drift.

It does not possess authorship over events. It does not choose outcomes, write history, reward virtue, or punish failure. Nevertheless, it meets enough conditions of reliable cosmic support that observers may perceive it as the specific hand of God holding every reality up.

## Stability without authorship

The growth current can appear providential without being a person or planner. Its stability allows worlds and systems to persist, but their particular events remain unauthored.

The religious statement—“a hand holds reality up”—may therefore describe a real pattern while incorrectly assigning intention to it.

Whether **growth current** and **growth force** are interchangeable names or distinct aspects remains open.

## Manifestation

The growth force does not manifest as intervention, and it is not a measurable field. It cannot be harmed, so it never needs defending, and it never punishes; the consequences of drift fall entirely on the drifters. It manifests as the long consequences of collective choices about circulation — legible only in history, at generational scale.

The clearest living example is [[World/Cultures/Ossuary States/00 Ossuary States Index|the Ossuary States]]. When they closed their borders, their people truly lived happily — happily enough that only a perceived idiot would leave, and only an actual idiot would permanently return. Drift produced no suffering to signal itself. And when they closed their borders, they kept their knowledge. A burned library is a wound the world knows it took; a hoarded one is a wound the world forgets it has. Once the hoarding outlasts living memory, the outside no longer knows what it is missing. The harm is not the loss but the loss of the knowledge of the loss.

[[World/Cultures/Hearthborn/The Missing Morph|The Hearthborn's missing morph]] and the Ossuary closure are paired warnings at species scale: one culture pruned away its invariant reference, one sealed its circulation and abandoned pruning altogether. Opposite failures, the same drift. Neither threatens the growth force itself; both harken to the grand structure without participating in its stakes.

## Self-pruning systems

The growth force encourages systems capable of self-pruning. Alignment is cyclical, and pruning is the return stroke: a system that can shed and correct is a system that can come back.

Current working interpretation:

- Sustainable growth requires internal limits.
- A stable system can shed, revise, or end growth that threatens the whole.
- Correction must arise from the system's own functioning rather than constant external authorship.
- Pruning is not automatically moral, wise, or correct.

Self-pruning must not be confused with purification, imposed extermination, or the assumption that whatever survives was cosmically preferred. The current authors no outcome and provides no moral permission.

[[World/Cultures/Hearthborn/The Missing Morph|The Hearthborn's missing morph]] is the defining warning. They pruned what appeared useless, but removed the invariant reference that allowed their corrections to remain healthy.

## Misalignment

A slow drift away from the growth current produces symptoms rather than a single clean catastrophe. Systems continue operating while becoming less able to recognize excess, error, instability, or unhealthy definitions of success.

Possible expressions already present in the world include:

- Hearthborn correction converging on an increasingly incorrect identity;
- Ossuary fear of processes without known upper limits;
- cultural progress retaining borrowed results while losing the boundary knowledge that made them sustainable; and
- institutions unable to distinguish hospitality from the absence of all protective limits.

These are thematic correspondences, not proof that the growth current directly caused each event.

## Open questions

- What minimum requirements cause a growth current to manifest?
- Is it one current spanning all realities or a recurring structure within each?
- Can it be measured, entered, resisted, or only inferred through system behavior?
- What distinguishes genuine self-pruning from a system destroying its own necessary differences?
