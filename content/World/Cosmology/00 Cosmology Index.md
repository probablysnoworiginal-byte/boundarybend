# Cosmology

## Core concepts

- [[The Greater Realm]] — a naturally uneven reality subject to drift and misalignment
- [[The Growth Current]] — stable, non-authoring conditions that favor systems capable of self-pruning
- [[The Original Travelers]] — visitors from an assumed other reality who once stabilized the realm and left cultural pieces behind

## Epistemic boundary

The setting does not currently contain a mapped multiverse. The player has only their own reality as a direct point of comparison and an assumed external reality from which the Original Travelers came.
