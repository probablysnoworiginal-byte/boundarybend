# Government and Isolation

## The Ossuary States

The current political structure is an extremely isolated city-state system resembling an ancient republic. This isolation is recent relative to skeleton civilization's immense age: **seventy years before the game**, the Ossuary States shut themselves off from the outside world.

> [!note] Canon correction
> The original concept said “roughly sixty years.” The later clarification establishes seventy years as the current figure.

- Foreigners are tolerated but rarely welcomed.
- Trade continues.
- Immigration is almost unheard of.
- Skeletons almost never leave the States.

The specific institutions, member states, offices, and cause of the closure have not yet been defined.

## Leaving and recycling

Skeletons who attempt to leave are typically killed and recycled by [[The Boundary Storm|a systematic storm]] surrounding the States' outer boundaries.

The government instituted the storm at some point, but the nature of that act remains unclear. The state may have conceived and created it, modified something that already existed, or adopted the byproduct of an event that happened to support isolation without possessing any higher-order purpose of its own.

The storm makes formal criminalization of emigration almost beside the point: the boundary itself enforces containment. This can coexist with the older claim that departure is treated as a personal tragedy rather than a crime, while still leaving the government's moral responsibility sharply unresolved.

## The best-kept secret

The current state of the outside world is the Ossuary government's most closely guarded secret. Its citizens are not merely prevented from leaving; they are denied an accurate understanding of what now exists beyond the storm.

What citizens are told, why the truth is dangerous to the government, and how much the state itself actually knows remain unresolved.

## Recognition beyond the borders

Skeletons have become exceptionally rare outside the Ossuary States. In a few places or specialized contexts, someone may recognize a skeleton at best. A typical person may have no reliable framework for identifying one.

Their rarity is caused by isolation and the lethal consequences of leaving, not by the species' extinction or recent emergence.

## The transparent people and opaque state

The government is the central contradiction in a society that believes truth should be visible. As individuals rejected concealment and made transparency a virtue, their state gradually became more secretive.

This opacity is especially unsettling around the outside world's condition, [[The Boundary Storm|the storm]], government laboratories, and other places where ancient natural processes may have been pushed past prudent limits. These are not necessarily simple instruments of evil; they reveal how even a patient civilization can rationalize containment and dangerous experiments as acts of stewardship.

## Political questions

The state definitively hides the current condition of the outside world. It may also conceal:

- known results of large-scale Cultivation;
- the full reason for the border closure;
- the storm's origin and true nature;
- discoveries beneath [[The Children's Hospital]];
- military cultivation practices;
- a present crisis; or
- several unrelated truths accumulated over an immortal bureaucracy's long life.

These possibilities remain open, not canon.
