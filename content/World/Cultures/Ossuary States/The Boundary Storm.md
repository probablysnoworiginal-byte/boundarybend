# The Boundary Storm

The **Boundary Storm** is the current descriptive name for a systematic storm surrounding the outer boundaries of the Ossuary States.

## Established function

- The storm encloses the isolated city-state system.
- Skeletons typically do not leave because the storm kills and recycles those who attempt to cross it.
- The Ossuary government instituted the storm at some point.
- The storm supports the government's isolation and control of information.

Exactly what “recycling” means physically has not yet been established.

## Uncertain origin

It is unclear whether the government directly conceived and created the storm.

At least two broad possibilities remain open:

1. The storm is an intentional government system designed or shaped to enforce the border.
2. The storm is a byproduct of some event or process that happens to support the government's goals without possessing higher-order purpose or agency.

These possibilities are not equivalent. The first makes the storm a constructed instrument. The second makes the government an opportunistic steward of an indifferent catastrophe. A mixture—an existing phenomenon deliberately stabilized, positioned, or maintained—is also possible.

## Information boundary

The storm separates citizens from knowledge as well as geography. [[Government and Isolation|The government]] keeps the outside world's present condition as its greatest secret.

The storm may therefore function as:

- a lethal physical boundary;
- a barrier to observation and communication;
- evidence of a concealed historical event; and
- a mechanism allowing the state to define what citizens believe exists outside.

Only the first and the government's secrecy are currently established. The other functions remain possibilities.

## Moral ambiguity

The state may not personally execute emigrants, but it knowingly maintains or benefits from a boundary that does. Whether this is defended as protection, containment, stewardship, necessity, or something else remains open.
