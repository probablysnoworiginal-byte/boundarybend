# The Ossuary States Through Three Ages

> [!warning]
> ## Navigational chronology, not a solved chronology
> The three camps organize canon. Clothing, exact architecture, gate machinery, and the storm's visible form remain deliberately open.

> [!time]
> **The constant:** family care, Cultivation, repair, and stewardship continue across all three eras.  
> **The change:** an open civilization becomes an enclosed information system within the last seventy years.

> [!era-distant]+ I · DISTANT PAST — OPEN CONTINUITY
> ### What is established
>
> - Skeleton civilization is among the oldest in the world.
> - The [[The Children's Hospital|Children’s Hospital]] has been continuously maintained for fifteen thousand years: repaired, never rebuilt.
> - Cities are tended across generations instead of discarded and replaced.
> - Cultivation, family responsibility, bone memory, medicine, and caution toward scale already shaped ordinary life.
> - For most of this history, the civilization was **not** sealed away.
>
> ### What this era feels like
>
> Continuity is public. The gates are open; stewardship is ordinary civic life; ancient does not mean ruined. Skeleton culture has already survived disasters of scale, but isolation has not yet become its answer.
>
> **Threads:** [[Biology and Cultivation]] · [[Family and Inheritance]] · [[Stewardship and Material Culture]] · [[Scale, War, and Catastrophe]]

> [!era-century]+ II · LAST HUNDRED YEARS — THE CLOSURE
> ### What is established
>
> - The Ossuary States shut themselves off **seventy years before the game**.
> - Trade continued, while immigration became almost unheard of and skeletons almost stopped leaving.
> - The government instituted or adopted the [[The Boundary Storm|Boundary Storm]] at some point.
> - Leaving became typically fatal: the storm kills and “recycles” skeletons who attempt to cross.
> - The state increasingly controlled what citizens knew about the outside world.
>
> ### What remains unresolved
>
> The cause of the closure is not known. The storm may predate it, accompany it, or follow it. The state may have built the storm, reshaped an existing phenomenon, or learned to benefit from an indifferent catastrophe.
>
> This is a **conceptual transition**, not a canon snapshot of the storm's creation.
>
> **Threads:** [[Government and Isolation]] · [[The Boundary Storm]]

> [!era-present]+ III · PRESENT — TRANSPARENT PEOPLE, OPAQUE STATE
> ### What is established
>
> - The States remain an isolated city-state system resembling an ancient republic.
> - Skeletons are now so rare beyond the border that ordinary people may not recognize one.
> - The Boundary Storm enforces containment while the government hides the outside world’s present condition.
> - Government laboratories, military Cultivation, the hospital’s depths, and the storm all sit near the boundary between stewardship and concealed scale.
> - Ordinary life has not stopped. Families still wait, physicians still garden, buildings are still repaired, and children still emerge from donated bone.
>
> ### The present tension
>
> The civilization is not “a fallen ancient empire.” It is alive, capable, caring, and politically trapped inside a protection that has become control. The culture’s old ethic survives; the state’s relationship to truth does not.
>
> **Threads:** [[00 Ossuary States Index]] · [[Government and Isolation]] · [[The Boundary Storm]] · [[The Children's Hospital]]
