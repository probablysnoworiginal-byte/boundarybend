# Ossuary Glossary

## Established terms

**Cultivation**  
The natural process through which donated bones, planted and tended in a nursery, produce a child after twenty years.

**Nursery**  
A deliberately maintained place where a child is cultivated. It is both biological environment and family home.

**Bone memory**  
A common shorthand for persistent habits or physical patterns retained by bones. These are explicitly not the former owner's memories.

**The Children's Hospital**  
An institution maintained continuously for fifteen thousand years, where families wait while children grow beneath the earth. Something poorly understood lies below it.

**Boundary Storm**  
Working name for the systematic storm surrounding the Ossuary States' outer boundaries. It typically kills and recycles skeletons who attempt to leave. The government instituted it, but may not have originally conceived or created the underlying phenomenon.

## Provisional terms

**The Ossuary States**  
Placeholder name for the extremely isolated, republic-like city-state system that closed itself to outsiders seventy years before the game.

**Skeletons**  
Current descriptive name for the species or people. It plainly describes their bodies but has not been confirmed as their own name.

**Immortal**  
Describes their indefinite lifespan and capacity to regrow every bone other than a skull reduced completely to dust. The limits and timescales of recovery still need definition.
