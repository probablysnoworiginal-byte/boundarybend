# The Children's Hospital

The Children's Hospital may be the oldest continuously maintained structure in skeleton civilization.

- It has never been abandoned.
- It has never been rebuilt, only repaired.
- For fifteen thousand years, families have waited there while children slowly grew beneath the earth.
- No one leaves unchanged.

## What lies beneath

Something beneath the hospital is not fully understood.

It has not been forgotten, worshipped, or destroyed. It is simply endured—with care, grief, curiosity, and fear in equal measure.

The nature of this presence or process remains intentionally unresolved. Its relationship to Cultivation, the hospital's age, the vanished civilizations, or the modern government's secrecy has not been established.

## Why it matters

The hospital concentrates the civilization's defining qualities in one place: patient family care, continuous architecture, medicine as stewardship, an ancient natural process, and fear of an unknown scale that cannot simply be conquered or removed.

Its deepest mystery remains unresolved.
