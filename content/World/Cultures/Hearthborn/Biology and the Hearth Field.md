# Biology and the Hearth Field

## A self-contained psychic ecosystem

The Hearthborn do not draw upon a naturally occurring or universal psychic field. Specialized biological structures distributed across the species collectively produce the conditions required for psychic interaction.

- The field exists only because Hearthborn biology creates it.
- Outside a Hearthborn population, the field does not exist.
- No individual Hearthborn possesses a complete psychic system.
- Communication is an emergent consequence of ordinary, automatic biological processes.

The Hearthborn therefore do not “cast telepathy.” Telepathic interaction is part of their normal physiology.

## Complementary morphs

Established functions include:

1. A morph whose organ maintains the field itself.
2. A morph that permits information and identity to propagate through the field.
3. A morph that continuously suppresses instability, including destructive resonance, identity bleed, and runaway psychic feedback.
4. The eliminated morph, whose isolation from the network supplied an invariant reference for identity. See [[The Missing Morph]].

These organs are no more consciously operated than a human heart or kidneys.

## Systemic consequence

The psychic ecosystem depends upon complementary bodies rather than a single gifted individual. A functioning Hearth is therefore simultaneously biological, social, and infrastructural, although the exact meaning and physical scale of “Hearth” still needs definition.
