# The Hearthborn

The Hearthborn are a young sapient species, approximately 400 years old, whose civilization developed around complementary biological morphs that collectively create a self-contained psychic ecosystem. The field is not a universal force they learned to exploit: Hearthborn bodies produce it, and it does not exist outside a Hearthborn population.

## Core premise

No individual contains a complete psychic system. Different morphs maintain the field, propagate information and identity, and suppress dangerous instability. These processes are automatic physiology, not consciously cast telepathy.

The Hearthborn eliminated a morph that appeared to contribute nothing to the network. In truth, its isolation was its purpose: it supplied an independent reference for identity. The network remained possible without it, but gradually lost the ability to determine whether its own corrections were healthy.

> [!key] Dramatic core
> The missing morph did not make the network possible. It made the network correct.
