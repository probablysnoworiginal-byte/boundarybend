# The Missing Morph

## Apparent uselessness

Long before the game, the Hearthborn eliminated an entire biological lineage. Its members appeared to provide no meaningful function within a Hearth:

- They generated no field.
- They relayed nothing.
- They stabilized nothing.
- Their organs had no network “address.”
- They could neither be called nor participate as other Hearthborn did.

Over generations, the lineage came to be regarded as unnecessary and was bred out of existence. Nothing immediately failed, so the decision appeared justified.

## Actual function

The missing morph was never supposed to participate in the network. Its organ existed outside the addressable system and maintained identity rather than communication.

It acted as an invariant point: a living, independent reference against which the rest of the psychic ecosystem unconsciously organized itself.

Without that reference, the network can still operate and correct itself. It can no longer reliably determine whether the definition toward which it is correcting is healthy or true.

## Name remains open

Four working names describe different parts of the function:

- Null
- Anchor
- Bootstrap
- Calibrator

None is yet the canonical name.

## Legacy

- The civilization's delayed consequences are documented in [[The Long Failure]].
- The player character is [[The Last Missing Morph|the last known surviving member]] of this lineage.
