# Hearthborn Glossary

## Established terms

**Hearthborn**  
A young sapient species whose complementary biological morphs collectively create a self-contained psychic ecosystem.

**Hearth field**  
A convenient descriptive phrase for the psychic environment generated by Hearthborn biology. It is not a universal external force and does not exist outside a Hearthborn population.

**Morph**  
A complementary biological lineage whose specialized organ performs one function within the Hearthborn psychic ecosystem. The precise social and reproductive meaning of morph remains to be developed.

**Suppressor**  
The morph or organ function that continuously limits destructive resonance, identity bleed, and runaway psychic feedback. This is a descriptive label, not necessarily the Hearthborn's own term.

**Hearth**  
A capitalized term used for a functioning Hearthborn collective or psychic system. Its exact scale—household, settlement, population, field boundary, or some combination—has not yet been defined.

## Working terms

**Null / Anchor / Bootstrap / Calibrator**  
Candidate descriptions for [[The Missing Morph|the missing morph's]] identity-reference function. No final term has been selected.

**Address**  
A descriptive way to explain whether an organ or individual can be called and incorporated by the network. Whether this is literal in-world terminology is undecided.
