# The Last Missing Morph

The Hearthborn player character is the last known member of [[The Missing Morph|the eliminated morph]].

## How he appears

- He is not stronger than other Hearthborn.
- He has no greater psychic power.
- His organ cannot be addressed by the network.
- He exists within the field without ever being incorporated into it.
- To his society, he appears deficient: evolution's mistake rather than its safeguard.

## What he actually carries

His apparent deficiency preserves an independent, irreducible reference for identity. His organ does not add a new ability to the network; it provides the external standard the network lost when his lineage disappeared.

He is therefore evolution's forgotten safeguard—not a psychic superweapon, but evidence that the Hearthborn's shared definition of health and identity can be wrong.

## Narrative function

His existence makes recognition possible. For the first time in generations, the Hearthborn may be able to compare consensus against something the network cannot absorb, revise, or explain away.

The circumstances of his survival and the limits of what his presence can repair remain open.
