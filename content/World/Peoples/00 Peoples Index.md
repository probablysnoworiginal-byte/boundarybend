# Peoples

This index tracks biological peoples independently from cultures, governments, and places. “Race,” “species,” and other in-world taxonomies have not yet been formally defined.

## Common in the Walking Haven

1. [[Humans]] — currently the most numerous residents and the setting's every-person perspective
2. [[Fish Folk]] — the second most numerous; highly diverse biological lines filtered by what environments gameplay can support
3. [[Unnamed Third Common People]] — the least numerous of the three, but socially ordinary and familiar

See [[World/Places/Walking Haven/Population|Walking Haven Population]].
