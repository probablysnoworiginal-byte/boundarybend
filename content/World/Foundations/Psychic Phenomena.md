# Psychic Phenomena

## Compatibility is not infrastructure

Most intelligent species possess nervous systems capable of interacting with psychic phenomena. That compatibility does not mean their bodies can safely create, regulate, or sustain psychic contact.

The [[World/Cultures/Hearthborn/00 Hearthborn Index|Hearthborn]] are unique among known intelligent species because their complementary biological morphs collectively provide a complete, self-contained psychic infrastructure.

## Humans

Humans can interact with psychic fields, but they evolved without the supporting organs necessary to regulate those interactions. Forced contact—through rituals, eldritch entities, or other means—exposes an ordinary human brain to conditions it was never designed to survive.

The resulting madness is biological overload, not magical punishment.

## Hearthborn distinction

Hearthborn psychic contact is safe under normal conditions because field generation, information propagation, and suppression evolved together as one distributed biological system. Their present crisis comes from the loss of an identity-reference function, not from the ordinary existence of the field. See [[World/Cultures/Hearthborn/The Long Failure|The Long Failure]].
