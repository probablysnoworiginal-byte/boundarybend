# World Through Three Ages

> [!time]
> ## One world, three useful distances
> This is a **navigation lens**, not a claim that every culture changes on the same schedule. Any historical thread can be placed in the camp that best explains its relationship to the player’s present—even when its exact date remains unresolved.

> [!multi-column]
>
>> [!era-distant]+ I · DISTANT PAST
>> **More than 100 years before the game**
>>
>> Origins, vanished solutions, ancient institutions, deep continuity, and the inheritance of events no living culture fully understands.
>
>> [!era-century]+ II · LAST HUNDRED YEARS
>> **The century that made the present**
>>
>> Closures, failures, policy changes, route collapse, and slow transformations whose witnesses may still exist.
>
>> [!era-present]+ III · PRESENT
>> **The world the player can touch**
>>
>> Current crises, living institutions, active secrets, and conditions that can answer back when the player acts.

## I · Distant past

These belong here because their **historical distance** matters more than an exact date.

- [[World/Cosmology/The Original Travelers|The Original Travelers]] crossed into the Greater Realm, stabilized a larger crisis, left cultural pieces behind, and departed. Their dates remain open.
- [[World/Places/Walking Haven/00 Walking Haven Index|The Walking Haven]] has offered refuge for thousands of years without institutional reset.
- [[World/Places/Walking Haven/Cultural Time Capsule|The Haven’s cultural time capsule]] preserves transitions that ordinary archives have lost.
- [[World/Cultures/Ossuary States/The Children's Hospital|The Children’s Hospital]] has been continuously maintained for fifteen thousand years.
- [[World/Cultures/Ossuary States/Scale, War, and Catastrophe|Ossuary catastrophes of scale]] taught an immortal civilization to fear the question “what if we keep going?”

## II · Last hundred years

This camp holds changes recent enough to define the world’s current political and experiential shape.

- [[World/Cultures/Ossuary States/Government and Isolation|The Ossuary States closed seventy years ago]]. Trade continued, but immigration nearly vanished and skeletons almost stopped leaving.
- [[World/Cultures/Ossuary States/The Boundary Storm|The Boundary Storm]] now makes departure lethal. Its exact origin—and whether it predates, accompanied, or followed closure—remains unresolved.
- The Ossuary government increasingly controlled what its citizens could know about the outside world.
- [[World/Cultures/Hearthborn/The Long Failure|The Hearthborn’s long failure]] accumulated across generations. It may straddle the boundary between this camp and the deeper past until its chronology is defined.
- The farthest routes tied to the [[World/Places/Traveler Legacy Hub/Protection and Fallen Routes|Traveler legacy network]] were the first to fail, but the exact sequence still needs dates.

## III · Present

This is not simply “modern lore.” It is the set of conditions capable of becoming **play**.

- [[World/Cosmology/The Greater Realm|The Greater Realm]] is drifting away from stable growth.
- The [[World/Places/Traveler Legacy Hub/00 Traveler Legacy Hub Index|protected legacy hub]] consumes news of distant problems while remaining insulated from them.
- The [[World/Cultures/Ossuary States/00 Ossuary States Index|Ossuary States]] remain sealed, and the government hides the outside world’s present condition.
- The [[World/Cultures/Hearthborn/00 Hearthborn Index|Hearthborn]] still appear prosperous even though their self-correcting system has lost its reference for health and identity.
- The player enters this inherited world through [[Game Design/Open World Structure|a failing network of boundaries and routes]].

## Questions that remain deliberately open

- When did the Original Travelers arrive and depart?
- What event made the Ossuary closure appear necessary seventy years ago?
- When was the Boundary Storm instituted relative to that closure?
- When did the Traveler routes begin failing, and how quickly did the failure spread?
- Which slow changes belong to the last century, and which only became *visible* during it?

These questions can move between camps as the chronology sharpens.
